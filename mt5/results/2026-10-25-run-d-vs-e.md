## 3. Where the zone misses land — do not widen the zone

This was the diagnostic built into the last build, and it answers the question directly. Of the 684 zone misses:

| Distance short of the zone | Share |
|---|---|
| ≤ 1 pip | 25.0% |
| ≤ 3 pips | 31.7% |
| ≤ 10 pips | 44.2% |
| ≤ 20 pips | 59.1% |

Median miss is **13.9 pips**, p90 is 55. Widening the 45–55% band slightly would rescue a quarter of them. Widening far enough to rescue half would turn the "zone" into "somewhere in the middle of the leg", which is not a 50% retracement any more.

**Recommendation: leave the zone at 45–55%.** The misses are mostly genuine — price reversed without retracing — not a rounding artefact.

## 4. Defect 1 — my confluence score silently repealed D-08

**This is the important one, and it is my error.**

D-08 was ratified as **"OB and FVG, both required"**. In §0j I proposed demoting them to a ranking score, and that was approved with option B. But the score I actually implemented is:

```
score = OB(1) + FVG(1) + zoneOverlap(1)
```

A score of 2 is produced by **either** `OB + FVG` **or** `OB + zoneOverlap` **or** `FVG + zoneOverlap`. So `InpMinConfluence=2` does **not** enforce "both required" — it happily admits a setup with only an order block. The score conflates *quality of confluence* with *presence of the ratified rule*.

In RUN D this mattered: 18 confirmed setups had only one of the two. **13 of the 39 signals came from those**, and all 13 are setups D-08 says should not trade.

**Fixed in the source since this run** — new input `InpZoneRequireOBFVG` (default `true`), enforcing OB and FVG as a rule in zone mode, kept strictly separate from the 0–3 quality score. **This needs one more compile.**

Expected effect: 39 → **26 or fewer** signals. Even at 26 that is still ×1.9 on option A, i.e. ~5.2/yr/pair and ~3.2 years to 200 trades. The zone entry is still clearly the right call.

If you would rather see the absolute ceiling before accepting that constraint, set `InpZoneRequireOBFVG=false` — but then the run is not implementing the ratified rules.

## 5. Defect 2 — repeat entries on one setup

7 of the 39 signals are second and third entries on the *same* setup, at effectively the same price:

| Setup | Entries | Prices |
|---|---|---|
| `down sweep@1.08533` | 2024.03.05 12:15, 12:45, 14:45 | 1.08465, 1.08463, 1.08457 |
| `down sweep@1.08594` | 2024.02.26 15:00, 15:15 | 1.08461, 1.08410 |
| `down sweep@1.09138` | 2024.11.04 14:30, 16:00 | 1.09008, 1.08989 |

The existing `duplicate setup` guard only blocks the *same bar*. In zone mode price sits in the zone for hours, so the engulfing condition keeps re-firing.

**This does not need a code change.** In live trading `HasPositionOnSymbol()` blocks the follow-ups, and `InpMaxConcurrent=2` and the 3/day cap back it up. It inflates the dry-run count only, which is why the honest frequency figure is **35 distinct setups (7.0/yr)**, not 39.

Worth knowing: 2024.03.05 fired 3 signals in one day, so in a live run the daily cap would bind there anyway.

## 6. Correction — I overstated the stop-width problem

I warned before the run that the stop would "roughly double" and the 1:3 target would move much further away. **Measured, it did not.**

| | RUN E | RUN D |
|---|---|---|
| SL median | 14.3 pips | 16.2 pips |
| SL at the 12-pip floor | most | 26% |

The reason is `InpMinSLPips = 12`. In option A the structural stop was often only 8–10 pips and got pushed out to the floor, so the floor was already setting the number. In zone mode the structural stop is naturally 12–20, so the floor stops masking it and the difference is small.

The warning still applies to the **tail**: 8% of signals carry a 45+ pip stop, and the worst is 60.9 pips → a 183-pip target. Those few trades will dominate any P&L and are the ones to watch.

---

## 7. What this means

The project's binding constraint was sample size: 6 years to a verdict. With the zone entry plus the D-08 gate, it is roughly **3 years across 12 pairs**, or **2 years if the 12-pair universe is traded concurrently with unique magic numbers**.

That is a viable research loop. It is the first time in this project that has been true.

**Still not evidence of an edge.** These are dry runs. 39 signals, no fills, no P&L. The next run must place real orders.
