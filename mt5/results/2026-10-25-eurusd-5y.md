## 2. Relaxing ER from 0.30 → 0.20 gave 14 signals, not the 30–60 predicted

**My prediction was wrong, and worth stating plainly.** I expected a 4–7× increase. The actual increase was 75% (8 → 14).

Reason: I assumed ER was a single dominant choke point. It is not. Relaxing it merely exposed the next gates.

### Funnel comparison

| Rule | ER 0.30 | ER 0.20 |
|---|---|---|
| **R3a efficiency ratio** | 31,759 (67.1%) | **22,665 (47.9%)** |
| **R3b no structure shift** | 6,621 (14.0%) | **10,419 (22.0%)** |
| **R2 no recent sweep** | 4,101 (8.7%) | **5,207 (11.0%)** |
| **R4 zone not reached** | 2,734 (5.8%) | **5,143 (10.9%)** |
| R3a swing density | 602 (1.3%) | 1,147 (2.4%) |
| R2 sweep violated | 1,549 (3.3%) | 1,549 (3.3%) |
| R5 engulf / POI / FVG | ~1,200 | ~1,100 |
| **Signals** | **8** | **14** |

**Read this as a compound-probability problem, not a knob problem.** Five sequential gates each passing 40–70% multiply to ~3%. There is no single threshold to tune; the *conjunction* is the constraint.

Signal rate at ER 0.20: **14 / 47,337 evaluations = 0.03%**, i.e. **2.8 signals per year per pair.**

## 3. The strategic conclusion

At 2.8 signals/year/pair, even the full 12-pair universe yields **~34 trades/year**. A 1:3 system needs ~200 trades to distinguish a 33% win rate from a 25% break-even.

> **200 trades ÷ 34/year ≈ 6 years of validation.**

That is not a viable research loop, and it is the single most important number produced by this test. The rule set as implemented is a genuinely low-frequency confluence model; it is not a strategy you can validate, iterate on, or forward-test in a reasonable time.

This is a sharper version of the warning in `decisions.md` §3 — my original estimate was 35–130 trades/year **pooled across 5 pairs**. The measured figure is ~14/year across 5 pairs. I was 2.5–9× too optimistic, because I assumed the MSS, engulf and POI gates were far less restrictive than they are.

## 4. What to change, in priority order

### 4.1 Implement option B from `decisions.md` §0j — enter *in* the 50% zone

This is both a **fidelity fix** and a **frequency fix**, which is unusual and is why it comes first:

- **Fidelity:** the checklist says "valid liquidity retracement (e.g. 50%)". Today the EA only requires the zone to have been *touched*, then enters at the POI, which sits near the sweep extreme. Both run-2 entries landed **below** the zone (`zone 1.16326-1.16355` vs `entry 1.16269`).
- **Frequency:** requiring price to reach the POI *after* the zone means waiting for a full retrace to the other end of the leg. Entering at the zone removes that extra journey, so more setups fill.

R4 alone blocks 10.9% of bars, and R5 blocks most of the rest with `engulf not in POI` / `OB and FVG do not overlap`. Moving the entry into the zone should convert a meaningful share of those into trades. **Expected: 2–3× more signals.** Measure, do not assume.

### 4.2 Run GBPJPY before concluding anything about the model

EURUSD is the least favourable instrument for this rule set: lowest ATR, most efficient price, and an ER distribution whose median is 0.14. JPY crosses have structurally higher ER and more sweep/MSS behaviour. **If GBPJPY produces 3–5× the signals, the model is fine and EURUSD was the wrong test.**

### 4.3 Do not tune ER further

0.20 is defensible. Dropping to 0.10–0.15 would buy maybe 4–6 more signals a year at a real cost in quality. The bottleneck is no longer ER.

### 4.4 The SL floor is doing visible work

Trade 1 opened with an **8.0-pip** stop — the `InpMinSLPips` floor, not structure. The run used 8.0 (the preset's 12.0 was not applied in these runs). At 12.0 that trade would not have been taken, or would have been sized differently. Keep 12.

## 5. Caveats on this data

- **History quality 24%** (tick gaps). Irrelevant for signal *detection* (the EA is bar-based), relevant for exit timing. Bar-data quality drives the funnel; tick quality drives fills.
- **Three runs in one file.** Anyone reading this log as a single run would report 28 signals and 98,522 rejections — both wrong by a factor of ~2.
- **n=6 on the live run.** No statistical claim of any kind can be made from it.
- The live run used `InpERMin = 0.30`, so it represents the *old* configuration. The 14-signal run never placed orders.
